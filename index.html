Documentation & Architecture

Command Grammar

The standard syntax recognized by the Grok action parser is:

UPload It. [NAME], [TICKER], [X_LINK], [WEBSITE]

Only NAME and TICKER are strictly mandatory. When X or Website links are omitted, a canonical landing page is provisioned automatically.

Fee Distribution Mechanics

Creation & Simulation: $0 platform fee. Memecoin metadata and Ed25519 mint keys are created free in Grok. Deploying live to Solana Mainnet costs only trivial Solana network gas (~0.02 SOL rent).

Trading Fees & Incentives: 80% of creator fees buy and burn $UP on-chain. When your coin bonds on pump.fun, you claim an instant 3 SOL creator milestone bonus, followed by 20% perpetual fee rev-share in SOL for as long as it trades.
